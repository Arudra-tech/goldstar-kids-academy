Ms. Denise A.

Founder's Story

Raising Every Child
to Their Fullest Potential

M.S. Sociology · M.Ed. Child Development & Education · Licensed Center Director
"I didn't just want to create a daycare. I wanted to build an environment where every single child feels seen, valued, and set up to succeed in life."

Denise's path to founding Goldstar Kids Academy was shaped by years of rigorous academic pursuit and an innate passion for understanding people. After earning her first Master's degree in Sociology, she developed a deep, research-driven perspective on how social environments — including the earliest ones children encounter — fundamentally shape identity, confidence, and long-term outcomes.

Driven to translate that academic lens into direct impact, she went on to earn a second Master's degree in Child Development and Education, specializing in early childhood learning environments and curriculum design. This rare combination of sociological theory and early childhood practice is what sets Goldstar apart: care here is never one-size-fits-all. It is intentional, individualized, and grounded in science.

Denise opened Goldstar Kids Academy in Freehold, NJ with a single mission — to give every child, regardless of background, the nurturing foundation and intellectual stimulation they deserve in their most formative years. She remains actively involved in the daily life of the center, knowing every child by name and working closely with families to ensure each child's unique strengths are celebrated.
